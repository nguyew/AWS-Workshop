## Overall Evaluation

### **1. Working Environment**
The internship environment at **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** through the **FCJ Program** was professional, well-structured, and highly supportive. The training sessions were organized effectively, making it easier to stay focused and gain practical knowledge. In addition, the workshops and technical sharing sessions provided valuable opportunities to explore AWS services and keep up with current cloud technologies.

### **2. Mentor and Program Support**
Throughout the internship, my mentor was always approachable, patient, and willing to explain technical concepts whenever I encountered difficulties. Instead of providing direct solutions, the mentor encouraged me to analyze problems independently before offering guidance, which significantly improved my critical thinking and problem-solving skills. The FCJ support team also handled administrative tasks efficiently and was always available to answer questions, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable internship experience.

### **3. Relevance to My Academic Background**
The internship closely aligned with my university studies while providing practical experience beyond the classroom. I enhanced my technical knowledge of AWS services and strengthened essential soft skills such as teamwork, communication, documentation, and technical presentation. Sessions delivered by AWS professionals and FCJ members also broadened my understanding of current industry practices and future career opportunities.

### **4. Learning and Professional Development**
This internship provided numerous opportunities to improve both technical and professional competencies. I gained practical experience with AWS cloud services, collaborated effectively within a team, and became more familiar with professional workflows. The guidance and career advice shared by my mentor also helped me gain a clearer understanding of the skills required for a career in Cloud Computing and DevOps.

### **5. Company Culture and Collaboration**
The working culture was open, collaborative, and respectful. Team members were always willing to share knowledge and support one another whenever challenges arose. Despite being an intern, I felt welcomed and valued as part of the team, which motivated me to participate actively and continuously improve myself.

### **6. Internship Program and Benefits**
The internship program was well organized and offered a valuable learning experience through technical workshops, training sessions, and knowledge-sharing activities. Flexible scheduling and continuous support from mentors made it easier to balance learning objectives with assigned responsibilities.

## Additional Questions

### **What was the most valuable part of your internship?**

The most rewarding aspect of the internship was the opportunity to gain practical experience with AWS cloud technologies through hands-on labs and workshops. Working directly with cloud services allowed me to strengthen my technical knowledge, improve my problem-solving skills, and better understand real-world cloud architecture and operations.

### **What improvements would you suggest for future internship programs?**

I believe the program could be enhanced by including additional team-based projects and more advanced practical scenarios that simulate real production environments. These activities would allow interns to apply what they have learned while improving collaboration and project management skills.

### **Would you recommend this internship to other students? Why?**

Absolutely. I would highly recommend this internship to students who are interested in Cloud Computing, AWS, or DevOps. The program provides a strong balance between theoretical knowledge and practical experience, supported by experienced mentors and a collaborative learning environment that encourages continuous growth.

## Suggestions and Future Expectations

### **Do you have any suggestions for improving the internship program?**

I recommend incorporating a comprehensive capstone project that combines multiple AWS services and requires teamwork throughout the development process. This would help interns integrate the knowledge gained during training while experiencing a workflow similar to real industry projects.

### **Would you like to continue participating in this program in the future?**

Yes. If given the opportunity, I would be excited to continue participating in the program. I hope to explore more advanced AWS topics, strengthen my cloud architecture and DevOps skills, and gain additional hands-on experience through larger and more challenging projects.
